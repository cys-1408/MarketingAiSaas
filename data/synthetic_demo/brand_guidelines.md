# Brand Guidelines (SYNTHETIC DEMO — fictional brand, for testing only)

## Tone of voice
- Clear, factual, and calm. Never hype.
- Address the user directly; avoid jargon.

## Terminology
- Say "workspace", not "account".
- Say "metric", not "KPI" in interface copy.

## Prohibited claims (must not claim)
- "Will convert more" / "guarantees results" — outcomes are never promised in copy.
- Do not compare to named competitors.
- Do not claim a metric improvement that has no supporting evidence.

## Disclaimers
- When citing numbers, name the source document and period.
- When proposing changes, label causal wording as a hypothesis until an experiment supports it.

## Channel rules
- Email: include an unsubscribe reminder in drafts.
- Paid social: no "free trial" language without legal approval (fictional rule).
